Our Story

From Environmental Crisis to Agricultural Gold

In Sukaregang, Garut, the leather tanning industry left behind a significant environmental footprint, polluting rivers and affecting local water supplies.

PT. Mandraguna Pusaka Indonesia saw an opportunity where others saw a problem. We innovatively process leather tanning waste—specifically rich animal fats—converting it into a high-value organic liquid fertilizer packed with amino acids.

"By resolving a decades-old pollution issue, we create superior organic products that promote healthier crops, richer soil, and a sustainable future for global agriculture."

Versatile Application

MANDRAGUNA GROW is formulated for maximum efficacy across various crop types. Perfect for large-scale agricultural exports.

Food Crops

Rice, Corn, Wheat.

  • Seed soaking: 10ml/L
  • Vegetative: 10ml/L
  • Generative: 20ml/L

Tubers

Potatoes, Cassava, Sweet Potatoes.

  • Vegetative: 10ml/L
  • Generative: 20ml/L
  • Apply every 10-14 days

Fruits & Veggies

Horticulture, leafy greens, orchard fruits.

  • Vegetative: 5ml/L
  • Generative: 10ml/L
  • Double dose for soil drench

Plantations

Palm oil, Rubber, Coffee, Cacao.

  • Vegetative: 10ml/L
  • Generative: 20ml/L
  • Ideal spraying: Morning/Late Afternoon
